# Desktop Workflow v1 Contract

1. Product Desktop uses the opaque `desktop_product` Runtime Context created by Desktop Product v1. Browser input never supplies Context, paths, canonical IDs, command tokens or fingerprints.
2. Startup of Data, Cockpit, Tax and Analysis is read-only. Data is the only canonical UI writer and the only UI that can start the fixed Workflow Worker after a redacted Plan and a maximum-ten-minute one-use confirmation.
3. The ten ordered stages are Canonical, FIFO/GuV, Tax, Grouping, Manual Market, Manual FX, Unrealized Valuation, Trade Analysis, Market Analysis and atomic Publication.
4. The schema-201 Product Ledger stays canonical. A read-only deterministic calculation projection creates an owner-only ephemeral schema-400 input; it is never a second editable source.
5. Full precedes Incremental. v1 executes the full oracle for both modes and records deterministic Full parity rather than claiming an optimization.
6. Tax selection and every Asset classification are explicit. The German reference plugin is optional, isolated and non-binding. Event and Annual Tax remain separate. A deliberately deselected Tax plugin (`tax_policy.mode = disabled`, Run Center "Nicht auswählen" or a scope without the tax report) is a choice, not a gap (rc.406, #564): the `tax` stage is `not_selected` (only this stage may carry it), Tax readiness is `not_selected`, valuation positions with a gain carry `tax_status = not_selected` and stay `completed` with projected Tax and net liquidation left empty (never zero, never Brutto presented as Netto), and Trade Analysis is not `partial` for that reason. A selected plugin that yields no projection stays a visible gap (`no_plugin`, `partial`). The same applies to the unrealized projection alone when the portfolio scope deselects the tax projection module (`tax_projection = false`, read-only from `portfolio-configuration-v1`): Event and Annual Tax are still computed, the valuation receives a `not_selected` Tax artifact.
7. Listing code, price, observation time, FX pair, direction, rate and observation time are manual reviewed evidence. Market currency may differ from FIFO basis currency. v1 accepts one explicit direct or inverse route per Asset against the Portfolio reporting currency and uses it only for exact matching component endpoints; unsupported multi-route cases remain unavailable. Conflicting same-currency evidence, graph search, Pair creation from symbols, fallback and 1:1 defaults are blocked.
8. Grouping is optional. v1 executes the existing no-Scheme Root/Asset projection; it does not invent a mandatory Scheme or memberships.
9. FIFO, Tax, Grouping, FX, Valuation and Analysis use their accepted domain implementations. Trade Analysis, Market Analysis and Group Projection remain distinct artifacts. FIFO keeps exact `quantity × unit price ± fee`; separately booked Trade Cash never replaces it. Composite Cash balances are independently summed with exact Decimal arithmetic from current canonical postings on internal `cash`/`bank` Accounts only, excluding external counterparties, and carry provenance `canonical_internal_cash_postings`. This preserves settlement Cash without misclassifying an internal Asset purchase as an external capital withdrawal.
10. Trade and Market artifacts may be published before the composite commit, but all Product display apps consume only the final composite `desktop_workflow_v1` latest pointer. Therefore they cannot show a mixed revision.
11. At run start and after ordered checkpoints, immutable progress revisions expose safe Stage states while preserving the prior model as `stale/running`. A failed attempt publishes `stale/blocked` with the failed Stage and registered safe code; without prior output it publishes `blocked` and no data. A successful attempt atomically publishes the new model. Prior data keeps its own Revision, As-of and completion provenance while stale.
12. Internal run state, evidence and Jobs are `0700`/`0600`, symlink-rejected and restart-safe. Exact completed or blocked Jobs replay without duplicate Domain work.
13. Product Browser models contain labels, ordinals, exact Decimals, dates, states and safe business provenance (including reviewed Listing, Market observation and FX direction/rate/observation) only. Paths, Claims, Context, SQL, internal IDs/refs and fingerprints are forbidden.
14. No stage grants Provider, network, Server/OIDC, mail, alarm, cron, deployment or private-data capability.
15. A new Plan may reuse the inputs of the last confirmed Job for a new As-of and Cutoff (#463): Portfolio, mode, Tax selection and rates, Church Tax, fee source and the per-Asset classification, partial exemption, fee overrides and Listing. Manual prices and FX routes belong to their own As-of; a Job with them, an unfinished Job or an inactive Portfolio is not reused. A bound completed valuation is not carried over; like any Plan without that binding, the new one takes market values from the valuation state. A new Asset gets the form defaults. A Plan repeats the last Job only when that Job completed after the installed program version with identical inputs and fingerprints; then no new Job is started.
